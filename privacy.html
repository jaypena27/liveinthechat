Privacy Policy

Live in the Chat Poster · Last updated November 17, 2026

Live in the Chat Poster ("the app") is a desktop tool run by Live in the Chat ("we", "us") on our own computer to upload short video clips we made to our own YouTube channel and TikTok account. It has no other users.

What the app accesses

YouTube (Google): when the YouTube account is connected, Google asks to approve the youtube.upload permission. The app uses it only to upload videos we choose and to set their title, description, tags and privacy. It does not read, change or delete other videos, comments, subscribers or account data.

TikTok: when the TikTok account is connected, TikTok asks to approve:

  • user.info.basic: the account's open ID, display name and avatar, to show which account is connected.
  • video.upload: send a video to the TikTok inbox as a draft, which we finish and post in the TikTok app.
  • video.publish (only if Direct Post is turned on): post a video to the profile with the caption and privacy we choose.

What we store and where

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens and the granted permissions are stored only on the computer running the app, in a local file. They are never sent to any server we run.
  • Video files and captions stay on that computer until they are uploaded.
  • We do not collect analytics, sell data, show ads, or share data with anyone. Data goes only to YouTube and TikTok to complete the uploads we request.

Google API Services

Live in the Chat Poster's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; by using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

  • Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected in the app (which deletes the local token file) or access is revoked: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, for TikTok in Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • TikTok refresh tokens expire after 180 days without use.
  • Videos we post are governed by YouTube's and TikTok's own privacy policies.

Children

The app is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
